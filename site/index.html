Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you start.

Does Pushti AI recognize Bangladeshi food?

The app supports AI classification of foods including Bangladeshi dishes. Recognition is approximate, and you should confirm the dish and serving size.

Can I connect a smartwatch?

With permission, Pushti AI can read compatible activity data shared through Android Health Connect. Device support depends on its companion app.

Does it work without the internet?

Some food logging and on-device recognition features can work offline. Server-based analysis and online nutrition lookup require internet connectivity.

Is Pushti AI medical advice?

No. Calorie and weight-goal calculations are estimates for general wellness, not medical diagnosis or individualized treatment.
